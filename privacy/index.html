Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TimeCo ("we" or "our") handles information when you use Calculator & Everyday Tools ("the app").

Information Stored on Your Device

The app does not require an account. Calculator expressions, results, calculation history, preferences, and saved converter layouts are stored locally on your device. We do not receive your calculations. You can delete local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Currency Rates

The currency converter downloads reference exchange rates from Frankfurter. Values you enter for conversion are calculated locally and are not sent to Frankfurter. Like any internet service, Frankfurter and its infrastructure providers may receive technical request information such as your IP address, device network information, and request time under their own policies.

Advertising and Consent

The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's advertising partners may automatically collect and share your IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), device and account identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, app and advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and performance data. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

In regions where consent or privacy choices are required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your choices are stored on your device and communicated to Google and relevant advertising partners. You can revisit these choices through the privacy options provided in the app's settings when available.

For more information, see Google's Privacy & Terms and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, the app opens your email application with your feedback text and a visible system-information block containing the app version, device brand and model, operating-system version, screen resolution, app locale, and time-zone offset. Nothing is sent until you choose to send the email. The message and your sender email address are then processed by your email provider and received by TimeCo for customer-support purposes. Feedback is optional and is not used for advertising or profiling.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.
